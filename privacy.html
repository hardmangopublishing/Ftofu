Privacy policy

What this site collects, what it does not, and how to have your details removed.

Email addresses

If you enter your email address to receive the free tofu rescue guide, that address is stored by the email service used to send it. It is used to deliver the guide and to send occasional recipes and new guides from this site.

Your address is never sold, rented or passed to anyone else. Every email includes an unsubscribe link, and unsubscribing removes you from the list permanently. You can also ask for your address to be deleted outright through the contact page.

Analytics and cookies

This site may use basic traffic analytics to understand which pages people read. That data is aggregated and does not identify individual readers.

Clicking an Amazon link sets a cookie in your browser that Amazon uses to attribute a purchase to this site. That cookie belongs to Amazon and is governed by Amazon's own privacy policy, not this one. It lasts twenty four hours. You can block or clear cookies through your browser settings at any time.

What is not collected

This site does not ask for names, addresses, phone numbers or payment details. No purchases happen here. Nothing you buy through an affiliate link is visible to this site beyond an anonymous commission report, which does not identify who bought what.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page. For any request about your data, including deletion, use the contact page. See also the affiliate disclosure.
