The 4 fixes that change everything

Master these and you’ll never serve (or eat) sad tofu again.

1

Press it properly

Remove the water. This single step is responsible for most texture failures.

2

Freeze then thaw

Creates a spongy, meatier structure that soaks up marinade like crazy.

3

High heat + fat

Crisp edges only happen with proper heat and a bit of oil. Air fryer or pan — both work.

4

Season like you mean it

Salt, soy, garlic, spices, acids. Treat it like the flavor carrier it is.
