Extra-Crispy Chili Garlic Tofu

This is the one most people point to when they say “okay, I get it now.” Sticky, spicy, crunchy.

Ingredients (serves 2–3)

  • 1 block (14–16 oz) extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 1.5 tbsp cornstarch or arrowroot
  • 2–3 tbsp neutral oil for cooking
  • 3–4 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1–2 tbsp chili garlic sauce or sambal (adjust heat)
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or honey
  • 1 tsp rice vinegar
  • Optional: green onions, sesame seeds

Method

  1. Press the tofu well (15–20 min). Cut into ¾-inch cubes or rectangles.
  2. Toss the tofu with cornstarch until lightly coated.
  3. Heat oil in a large non-stick or cast-iron pan over medium-high. Add tofu in a single layer. Cook undisturbed 3–4 minutes until golden, then flip and continue until crispy on most sides (8–10 min total).
  4. Push tofu to the side. Add garlic and cook 20–30 seconds. Add chili garlic sauce, soy sauce, maple, and vinegar. Toss everything together until the sauce thickens and coats the tofu (1–2 min).
  5. Finish with green onions and sesame seeds if using. Serve over rice, noodles, or with roasted vegetables.

Air fryer option: Toss tofu with a little oil + cornstarch, air fry at 390°F for 14–16 minutes, shaking halfway. Then toss in the sauce on the stovetop for 1 minute.

Smoky Paprika Tofu

Deep, savory, slightly sweet. Excellent warm or cold the next day in bowls and wraps.

Ingredients

  • 1 block extra-firm tofu, pressed (or freeze-thawed for best texture)
  • 1.5 tbsp olive oil or avocado oil
  • 1.5 tsp smoked paprika
  • 1 tsp garlic powder
  • ½ tsp onion powder
  • ¾ tsp salt
  • ½ tsp black pepper
  • 1 tsp maple syrup (optional but recommended)
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce

Method

  1. Press tofu. Cut into cubes, slabs, or tear into irregular pieces for more texture.
  2. In a bowl, mix oil, smoked paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, salt, pepper, maple, and soy sauce into a paste.
  3. Toss tofu thoroughly so every piece is coated. Let sit 15+ minutes if you have time.
  4. Cook: Pan-fry over medium-high until deeply browned, or air fry / roast at 400°F for 18–22 minutes, flipping once.
  5. Serve in bowls with greens, grains, avocado, or whatever you like. Also great in tacos or salads.

Ginger Soy Glazed Tofu

Minimal ingredients, maximum payoff. A reliable entry-point recipe.

Ingredients

  • 1 block extra-firm tofu, pressed
  • 2 tbsp soy sauce or tamari
  • 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger (or 1 tsp ground)
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 1 tbsp maple syrup or brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sesame oil
  • 1–2 tbsp neutral oil for cooking
  • Optional: chili flakes, sesame seeds, green onion

Method

  1. Press and cube the tofu.
  2. Whisk soy sauce, ginger, garlic, maple, and sesame oil together.
  3. Pan-fry tofu in neutral oil until golden and crisp on the edges.
  4. Pour the sauce over the tofu and cook 1–2 more minutes, tossing, until the glaze thickens and coats everything.
  5. Top with sesame seeds and green onion. Serve with rice and a simple vegetable.

Basic Crispy Tofu (blank canvas)

Once you can make plain crispy tofu well, you can sauce it any way you want.

Method

  1. Press tofu thoroughly. Cut into even cubes or sticks.
  2. Optional but recommended: toss with 1–2 tsp cornstarch and a pinch of salt.
  3. Heat a good amount of oil in a wide pan over medium-high.
  4. Add tofu in a single layer. Leave it alone for several minutes so a crust forms.
  5. Flip and continue until most sides are golden and crisp.
  6. Season with salt right as it comes out of the pan. Now it’s ready for any sauce or seasoning blend.

Tools that help these recipes succeed

A few items make a noticeable difference:

  • Tofu press – for better texture
  • Air fryer – easiest crispy results
  • Cornstarch – for extra crunch
  • Chili garlic sauce – for the chili garlic recipe
